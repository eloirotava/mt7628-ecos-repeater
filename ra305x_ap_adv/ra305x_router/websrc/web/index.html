SMART PLUG

Your Wi-Fi, your way.

Use an Ethernet uplink or extend an existing wireless network.

Save & apply keeps your settings after restart. Apply temporarily to try a configuration for this session.

Operating mode
Your Wi-Fi network WPA2 security. Enter 8–63 ASCII characters. Existing passwords are never displayed. Choose a channel for your access point.
Wi-Fi uplink

Avoid a network loop: disconnect the Ethernet uplink before applying Repeater mode. Keep access through the device's Wi-Fi.

Use the same channel as the existing network. WPA3-only networks are not supported.
